Before you RSVP

Formal Attire Required

We would love for our guests to embrace the formality of the evening and dress accordingly.

Men

  • Suit and tie required.
  • We encourage rich, darker tones such as black, navy, burgundy, plum, emerald, charcoal, and other similarly refined colors.

Women

  • Ankle-length or floor-length dresses, formal skirts, or elegant jumpsuits are requested.
  • Evening fabrics such as satin, silk, velvet, chiffon, crepe, and similar materials are encouraged.
  • Cutouts, slits, and lower or more dramatic necklines are perfectly welcome when styled tastefully.
  • Please avoid casual attire and bold or overly busy patterns. Subtle patterns, texture, and elegant detailing are welcome.

Dress Beautifully, Not Expensively

Formal attire does not have to mean an expensive outfit.

We want everyone to dress up and enjoy the occasion, but we absolutely do not expect our guests to spend a fortune to do so. There are wonderful options for affordable, discounted, vintage, and pre-loved formalwear.

We've gathered a few of our favorite places to help make finding something easy.

Wedding Party

Your role, details, and wedding-day information are below.

I want this to be a relaxed girls' getaway, not a stereotypical bachelorette party.

Vibe: Elegant & cozy while a little loose.

The basic idea is to rent one nice place where we can spend most of our time together: BBQ, casually drink, use the hot tub, play board/card games, have an at-home spa night, sit outside with coffee in the morning, talk, laugh and enjoy being together.

I don't need a packed itinerary. I'm happiest hanging out and partying with the people I'm close to.

Maximum length: Two/Three nights.

Guest List

Invited:

[LIST TO BE PROVIDED]

One bridesmaid will POSSIBLY be traveling from Saskatchewan, Canada. This depends on if I decide to have the party close to the wedding or not. Because she is already taking on the expense of traveling for the wedding, she is not to pay anything toward the bachelorette party. Her portion should be divided among the rest of the group.

If a guest cannot attend the proposed dates, talk to me before finalizing anything.

Timing

I'm debating on the bachelorette party being a few days before the wedding, primarily because one of my bridesmaids will already be traveling from Canada for the wedding. Or having it a month prior and just not having her come to that.

Either way, it should NOT be immediately before the wedding in a way that risks anyone being exhausted or hungover for the rehearsal or wedding.

I am very flexible about the specific dates.

Unavailable dates:
All birthdays and anniversaries on my conflict list should be avoided.

[DATE/CONFLICT LIST TO BE PROVIDED]

Everything should be substantially planned and booked at least three months beforehand.

Location

Keep it local.

Our group is primarily spread between Bakersfield and Simi Valley, California, so anywhere reasonably within that general area works.

Budget

Affordability is a priority. Find the best deals possible.

I am not personally budgeting money toward the bachelorette party, and the group will cover my portion.

My bridesmaid traveling from Saskatchewan should also pay nothing toward the bachelorette party if she attends.

Therefore, the costs for both:

  • The bride
  • The bridesmaid traveling from Saskatchewan

should be divided among the other participating guests.

Expenses may include:

  • Lodging
  • Groceries/meals
  • Transportation-related shared expenses
  • Optional activity/reservation costs

Alcohol can be BYOB so everyone can purchase whatever they personally want.

Before anything nonrefundable is booked, give me a few overall options to choose from.

Lodging

Preferred options, roughly in order:

  1. A resort, if we can find an unusually good deal
  2. Airbnb/vacation house
  3. Cabin or similar private rental

The property itself matters more to me than having lots of activities nearby because I'm completely happy spending the entire trip there.

Must-haves

  • Hot tub
  • Private or reasonably secluded
  • Full kitchen
  • BBQ/grill
  • Comfortable outdoor space
  • Walkable/safe area
  • Enough actual beds for the group

Sleeping arrangements

I am comfortable sharing a bed with my mother.

I would like to share a room with my mother and Julie, if the property allows it comfortably.

Everyone else should ideally have their own bed.

Major bonus

Pool table

A pool table isn't required, but I would LOVE one.

Transportation

The goal is to avoid unnecessary driving once we're there.

We can coordinate rides and share vehicles, probably primarily using my car and my mom's car.

If someone would rather drive herself separately, that's completely fine.

If we leave the property for an activity, I would strongly prefer somewhere within walking distance so nobody needs to drive after drinking.

Food

I would rather cook at the house, order delivery or get takeout than build the weekend around restaurants.

Breakfast

I'd love relaxed early breakfasts/coffee outside.

Breakfast groceries could include:

  • Eggs
  • Sausage
  • Sourdough bread
  • Fruit
  • Coffee
  • Other simple breakfast items

Dinner

BBQ — preferably tri-tip for me.

Sides and other food can be kept relatively simple.

Dietary restriction

I am lactose intolerant, so please make sure there are dairy-free/lactose-free options for me.

Drinks

Drinking is absolutely fine, but this isn't meant to be a drinking marathon.

Alcohol vibe: Casual/moderate.

Everyone can BYOB.

For me:

  • Martinis
  • Water
  • 100% pure cranberry juice

I want everyone to have fun, but I specifically don't want us destroying ourselves a few days before the wedding.

I'll probably naturally be ready for bed around 11:00PM.

Activities

Things I definitely want:

  • Hot tub
  • Board games
  • Card games
  • BBQ
  • At-home spa night
  • Relaxing together
  • Early breakfast/coffee outside

Major bonus

Pool table

Optional special activity

A winery could be fun if there's a good one within walking distance of where we're staying.

However, it is completely optional.

Do not choose more expensive lodging or significantly increase everyone's cost just to make a winery happen. I'm perfectly happy staying at the house the entire time.

If the budget allows for one special activity, a winery or something similar would be something I'd consider. No bars.

Spa Night

I'd love an at-home spa night rather than paying for an expensive spa outing.

Keep it relaxed: face masks, nails, skincare, comfortable clothes, drinks, music, talking, etc.

Nothing elaborate is necessary.

Games

I do not want traditional bachelorette-party games.

I'd much rather bring actual:

  • Board games
  • Card games

However, games involving Steven and me or questions/trivia about our relationship could actually be fun.

I'm fine being put on the spot.

Mildly cheeky is completely okay.

Theme & Decorations

No theme.

I don't need decorations.

If you really want to add a few decorative touches, keep them understated and elegant:

  • Cool-toned blue
  • Roses
  • Lace

That's plenty.

Absolutely no:

  • Penis decorations
  • Tacky bachelorette decorations
  • Over-the-top “BRIDE” everything
  • Matching outfits
  • Sashes/crowns
  • Classless or raunchy décor

I might be okay with a simple veil. That's about as “bachelorette” as I want to look.

Clothing

Everyone should just wear what they're comfortable in and what makes sense for whatever we're doing.

Gifts for the Girls

I want to give each woman one of the bridesmaid journals I've selected.

Custom Journal

These are gifts from me to them, not party favors they need to pay for.

Nightlife

No nightlife.

I do not want:

  • Clubs
  • Bar hopping
  • Strippers
  • Sexual entertainment
  • Raunchy activities
  • Classless bachelorette-party stuff

Surprises

Surprises are completely fine.

You don't need my approval for individual details as long as everything stays within the overall boundaries in this document.

Nothing raunchy, classless or wildly outside the vibe.

How Much I Want to Be Involved

I do not want to plan my own bachelorette party.

I'd like the Matron of Honor/bridesmaids to do the research and planning and then bring me a few good overall choices.

For example:

Option A: This house, these dates, approximately this much per person.

Option B: This resort, these dates, approximately this much per person.

Option C: This house near a winery, these dates, approximately this much per person.

I'll choose from the options.

After that, you can handle the details.

You can create a group chat and coordinate everyone without involving me in all the planning chatter.

Please handle cost discussions without me.

I don't mind receiving the final itinerary, but I also don't need every detail. I'm perfectly happy being told what to pack, where we're going and when to show up.

Information Still Needed From Me

Before planning begins, I will provide:

  • Final invitation list
  • Do-not-invite list
  • List of people whose attendance is essential
  • Birthdays/anniversaries and other dates to avoid
  • Emergency/contact information, including who to contact if Steven needs to be reached

The Most Important Rule

Don't over-plan it.

Give us a beautiful, comfortable place with a hot tub, food, drinks, games and privacy and let us enjoy being together.

The goal isn't to manufacture a huge “bachelorette experience.”

I want a cozy two-night girls' getaway with the women I love before I get married.

Your presence is the greatest gift

Wedding Registry

For those who have asked, you can find our registry below.

Please read before viewing our registry

We’re incredibly grateful for anything you choose to give us as we begin this next chapter together.

Our Amazon registry includes a few things we know we would love and use, but much of what makes a home feel like ours is a little harder to put on a traditional registry. We love pieces with history, character, and charm.

Because of that, if you would like to give outside of our registry, a monetary gift is genuinely one of the most helpful options. It allows us to search antique shops, estate sales, vintage markets, and secondhand treasures for pieces that feel special to us, while also making your gift stretch as far as possible.

We love the idea of building our home slowly, thoughtfully, and with pieces that each have a little story behind them.

Of course, your presence at our wedding is the greatest gift of all. We’re simply grateful to celebrate with the people we love.
